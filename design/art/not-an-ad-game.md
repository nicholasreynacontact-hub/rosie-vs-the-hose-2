# Not an ad-game: the line we are steering by

The user, after seeing the painted-garden build: *"I really don't want it to look like
one of those games that people get spammed to play in ads. I want it to truly feel like
an indie game experience."*

This note exists because "make it prettier" is not actionable and "indie" is not a style.
Both looks use bright colour and stylised shapes. What separates them is **restraint and
point of view**. Every item below is a concrete, checkable difference. Where the current
build sits on the wrong side, it is named.

## The tells, and where we are guilty

| Tell | Ad-game | Indie | Current build |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chroma** | Everything near-maximum saturation | A few saturated accents against many muted tones | **Guilty.** Magenta sky, coral deck, lime lawn, all at once |
| **Value range** | Uniformly bright, nothing dark | Real darks; light is a composition tool | **Guilty.** Almost nothing in the frame is dark |
| **Palette width** | Every hue present | A committed, narrow palette | **Guilty.** Bunting alone carries six hues |
| **UI** | Big glossy bevelled buttons, thick borders, drop shadows, labels shouting | Quiet, small, recedes, often part of the world | **Very guilty.** Four fat coloured discs and a row of chunky tags |
| **Effects** | Sparkles and particles constantly | Effects mark moments; stillness between them | **Partly guilty.** Pollen, sparkles and dapple run nonstop |
| **Surfaces** | Clean plastic gradients | Texture, grain, brush marks, imperfection | Mixed. Foliage is painted; every hard surface is clean |
| **Edges** | Hard outlines on everything | Edges found and lost in light | **Guilty.** Ink outlines on all characters |
| **Shapes** | Chunky, rounded, toy-like, symmetrical | Irregular, varied, specific | Mixed |
| **Type** | Heavy outlined novelty faces | Restrained, well-set, confident | **Guilty.** Stroked display type in the HUD |
| **Composition** | Everything centred and equally weighted | Depth, framing, breathing room | Improving — framing leaves help |

## What to do about it

1. **Cut chroma hard.** One or two saturated accents only, reserved for the things that
   must be read instantly: the weak spot, the danger warnings. Everything else muted.
2. **Let the frame go dark.** Deep shadow under planting, in the porch, at the edges.
   A picture with no darks reads as plastic.
3. **Narrow the palette.** Sage, moss, cream, warm stone, dusty rose, one gold. Retire
   the rainbow bunting or mute it to faded linen.
4. **Make the UI recede.** Smaller, flatter, lower contrast, no gloss or bevel, no heavy
   drop shadows. Controls fade toward transparent when untouched. The health read can
   live in the world rather than in a bar wherever possible.
5. **Ration the effects.** Particles earn their place by marking an event. Ambient
   sparkle is the clearest ad-game tell in motion.
6. **Texture every surface.** No clean gradient planes. Brush marks, grain, wear.
7. **Remove the ink outlines entirely**, as the user has ruled.
8. **Set the type properly.** No stroke, no novelty weight in the HUD; let the display
   face appear once, on the title.

## The test

Screenshot the game with the HUD hidden. If it looks like a frame from a film, it is
working. If it looks like a mobile ad, something above has crept back in.
