# Rosie vs. the Hose: Art Bible

Direction: **golden-hour toy diorama**. A cosy, mischievous backyard that looks like a
hand-painted toy set at sunset. Stylised and moody in colour, never horror.

## Visual pillars
1. **Toy-box shapes.** Everything is built from chunky rounded primitives with big heads,
   big eyes and short limbs. Mountains, trees and rocks are flat-shaded low-poly.
2. **Light is the paint.** A low warm sun against cool violet shadows, glowing lanterns and
   string lights, drifting pollen. Colour shifts tell the story of the fight.
3. **Made by hand.** Ink outlines, cel bands, paper-tag UI set slightly crooked, film grain.

## Shading rules
- Every surface is cel-shaded with a 4-band ramp (41%, 67%, 87%, 100%).
- Ink outlines on characters and hero props only: Rosie, the waterer, the hose, the weak
  spot, hose heads, sky creatures, treats, pot bodies, the dog house. Scenery stays unlined
  so the eye goes to what matters.
- Outline width grows with distance so the line reads the same near and far.

## Lighting and atmosphere
- Sun sits low behind and to the left, so shadows stretch toward the camera.
- Eight lanterns and the pergola string lights give warm pools at ground level.
- 150 drifting pollen motes, warm haze on the mountains.
- Screen finish: slight saturation and contrast lift, warm-violet vignette, light film grain.

## Characters
- **Rosie:** round fluffy body, oversized round head, wild tufts, large glossy eyes with two
  highlights, pink cheeks, red collar with a gold tag.
- **Waterer:** toy proportions, large eyes with brows, straw hat, yellow boots.
- **Hose heads:** white eyes, red pupils, slanted brows. Angry, not scary.
- **Sky creatures:** simple and friendly; sharks have teeth but round bodies.

## Environment dressing
Grass tufts, stepping-stone path, rocks, lanterns, bunting, a clothesline with swaying
laundry, Rosie's dog house with a painted sign, a picnic blanket, mushrooms.

## Do not
- No horror, gore, jump scares or dark desaturated palettes.
- No characters, logos or layouts taken from other games.
- No outlines on scenery; no pure black or pure grey.
