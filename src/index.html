Rosie vs. the Hose

The hose has taken over the yard. Hunt its weak spot across the whole deck, lawn and garden, time your bites, and dodge the water before Rosie gets soaked.

Move
Arrow keys or WASD, drag the joystick, or hold a spot on the ground
Bite
Space or J. Only the glowing yellow weak spot can be bitten. It leaps away until it runs out of breath. When it says BITE!, chomp it three times fast for a big RIP
Dash
Shift or K. Dash through an attack for a Perfect Dodge
Watch
Red lines, yellow cones and red circles mean water is coming. The yellow arrow points to the weak spot
Power
Bites and perfect dodges charge BOOGA TIME
